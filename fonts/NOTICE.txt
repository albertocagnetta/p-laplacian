Poster Roman Regular, Poster Roman Italic, and Poster Math are renamed WOFF2
subsets of Latin Modern Roman 10 Regular, Latin Modern Roman 10 Italic,
and Latin Modern Math. The changes are glyph subsetting, renaming, and format
conversion, prepared for this poster. The modified versions are distributed
under the GUST Font License and LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c or later.

Latin Modern Roman copyright 2003–2021 B. Jackowski and J.M. Nowacki
on behalf of TeX Users Groups. Latin Modern Math extensions copyright
2012–2014 B. Jackowski, P. Strzelczyk and P. Pianowski on behalf of TeX
Users Groups.

Originals: https://ctan.org/pkg/lm and https://ctan.org/pkg/lm-math
